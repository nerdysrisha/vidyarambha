ॐ
ఓం నమో నారాయణాయ
Kshama Prarthana
క్షమా ప్రార్థన

A humble prayer for forgiveness, traditionally recited at the close of daily worship. It asks the Lord to pardon all omissions and excesses committed during mantra, tantra, svara and varna recitation, and all errors of body, speech and mind. The prayer includes the sankalpam for the Achyuta-Ananta-Govinda nama japa as atonement, followed by the surrender verse "Kayena vacha manasendriyairva" and the closing "Harih Om Tatsat."
