ॐ
ఓం చాముండాయై నమః
Siddha Kunjika Strotram
సిద్ధ కుంజికా స్తోత్రం

A powerful hymn to the Divine Mother from the Rudrayamala (Gauri Tantra), in the form of a dialogue between Shiva and Parvati. Called the "key" (kunjika) to the Chandi Saptashati, it is said that reciting this stotra alone grants the fruit of chanting the entire Durga Saptashati, without the need for kavacha, argala, kilaka, nyasa or worship. Traditionally recited for swift siddhi in the Devi's mantra, protection, and fulfilment of all wishes; it is kept especially secret and should not be given to the faithless.
