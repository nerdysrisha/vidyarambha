ఓం నమో వేంకటేశాయ
Govinda Namavali
శ్రీవేంకటేశ్వర గోవింద నామములు

The sacred names of Lord Govinda, chanted at Tirumala and in Vaishnava homes as a namavali — each name followed by the refrain "Govinda." The names praise Venkateswara as the refuge of devotees, the slayer of demons, the source of all avatars, and the giver of both worldly and spiritual fruit. Recited for protection, peace and the grace of Lord Srinivasa.
