Shri Suktam
శ్రీ సూక్తం

A hymn to Goddess Lakshmi from the Rig Veda Khila, recited for the attainment of wealth, prosperity, fame and auspiciousness. It opens with the celebrated verse "Hiranyavarnam harinim suvarnarajatasrajam" — invoking Lakshmi of golden hue, adorned in gold and silver, who dwells in the lotus. The suktam praises her as the giver of cattle, horses, food, progeny and long life, and prays for the removal of Alakshmi (poverty and misfortune), for the indwelling of Shri in the home, and for the grace of the Divine Mother.
