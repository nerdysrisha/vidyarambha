ఓం కాలభైరవాయ నమః
Kala Bhairava Ashtakam
శ్రీ కాలభైరవాష్టకం

An eight-verse hymn in praise of Lord Kala Bhairava, the fierce and protective form of Lord Shiva. Traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya, it glorifies the Lord of Kashi, who dispels fear, destroys ignorance and guides devotees towards liberation. Reciting this sacred hymn is believed to invoke divine protection, courage and spiritual wisdom.
