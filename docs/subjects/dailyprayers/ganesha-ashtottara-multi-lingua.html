ॐ
ఓం గజాననాయ నమః
Sri Ganesha (Vinayaka) Ashtottara Shatanamavali
శ్రీ వినాయక అష్టోత్తర శతనామావళి

The 108 sacred names of Lord Ganesha (Vinayaka), the remover of obstacles, chanted as a namavali — each name prefixed with "Om" and followed by "namah." Traditionally recited at the start of any new undertaking and on Ganesha Chaturthi, for removing obstacles and gaining wisdom and success. A list of extra names is included at the end.
