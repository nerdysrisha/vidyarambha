Apaduddharaka Sri Rama Stotram
ఆపదుద్ధారక శ్రీరామస్తోత్రం

A prayer to Lord Rama as the remover of dangers and the giver of all wealth, invoking his protection along with Lakshmana. It includes the names of Vishnu and the Achyuta-Ananta-Govinda healing chant, praises Rama as the refuge of the distressed, and closes with verses of surrender, forgiveness and the Narayana prayer. Recited for protection from fear, disease and misfortune, and for the constant remembrance of the Lord.
