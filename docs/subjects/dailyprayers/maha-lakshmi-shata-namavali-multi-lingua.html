ఓం శ్రీ మహాలక్ష్మ్యై నమః
Maha Lakshmi Ashtottara Shata Namavali
శ్రీ మహా లక్ష్మీ అష్టోత్తర శత నామావళి

The 108 sacred names of Goddess Mahalakshmi, chanted as a namavali — each name prefixed with "Om" and followed by "namah." The names praise her as Prakriti, Vidya, Padma, Lakshmi, Kamala, Rama, Indira, the giver of wealth, wisdom, food, and liberation. Traditionally recited for prosperity, removal of poverty and sorrow, and the grace of the Divine Mother.
