Shri Bilvashtakam
శ్రీ బిల్వాష్టకం

A sacred hymn offering the bilva leaf to Lord Shiva, traditionally chanted during Bilva Puja and Shivaratri. Each verse ends with the refrain "Ekabilvam Shivarpanam" — the offering of a single bilva leaf, which the stotram declares equal to vast charities, pilgrimages, and yajnas. It praises the three-lobed bilva leaf as the form of the three gunas, the three eyes and the three weapons of Shiva, and promises the devotee liberation from the sins of three births.
