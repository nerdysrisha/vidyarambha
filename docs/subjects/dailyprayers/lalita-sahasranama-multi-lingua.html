Lalita Sahasranama
శ్రీ లలితా సహస్రనామ స్తోత్రం

The thousand sacred names of Goddess Lalita Tripura Sundari, the Divine Mother and embodiment of Shakti. Found in the Brahmanda Purana, this revered hymn is taught by Hayagriva to Sage Agastya. Devotees chant it to seek the Divine Mother's blessings, grace, protection, peace and spiritual wisdom. It is especially cherished during Navaratri and Friday worship.
