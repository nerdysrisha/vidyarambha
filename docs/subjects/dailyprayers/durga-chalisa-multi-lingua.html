Shri Durga Kavacham
శ్రీ దుర్గా కవచం

A protective armour (kavacham) of Goddess Durga from the Kubjika Tantra, spoken by Lord Shiva to the Goddess. Each verse assigns a form of the Divine Mother — Uma, Shuladharini, Khechari, Chandika, Simhavahini and others — to guard a specific part of the body, from head to feet. Recited for protection from all dangers, removal of fear, and the attainment of all siddhis; the text warns that chanting Durga's mantra without this kavacham yields no fruit.
