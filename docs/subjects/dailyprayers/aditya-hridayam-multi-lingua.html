Aditya Hridayam
శ్రీ ఆదిత్య హృదయం

A sacred hymn dedicated to Lord Surya, the Sun God, found in the Yuddha Kanda of the Valmiki Ramayana. Sage Agastya teaches this powerful prayer to Lord Rama before his battle with Ravana, inspiring courage, confidence and renewed strength. Traditionally recited for vitality, clarity, success and divine blessings, it celebrates the Sun as the source of light, life and cosmic energy. It is especially auspicious when chanted at sunrise.
