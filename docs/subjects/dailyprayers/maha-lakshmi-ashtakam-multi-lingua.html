Maha Lakshmyashtakam
మహా లక్ష్మ్యష్టకం

A hymn of eight verses in praise of Goddess Mahalakshmi, recited by Indra according to tradition. It extols her as the great illusion (Mahamaya), the dweller of Shri Peetha, the one who holds conch, discus and mace, and the rider on Garuda. Chanted for wealth, wisdom, removal of sins and sorrows, and the boons of both enjoyment (bhukti) and liberation (mukti), it praises her as Adi Shakti, Para Brahman, and the Mother of the Universe.
