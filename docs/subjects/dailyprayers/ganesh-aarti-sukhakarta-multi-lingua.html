Ganesh Aarti
శ్రీ గణేశ ఆరతి

A beloved Marathi devotional aarti dedicated to Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles and the divine harbinger of auspicious beginnings. The traditional “Sukhkarta Dukhharta” aarti praises Lord Ganesha's compassion, beauty and benevolent grace. Devotees sing it to seek blessings for wisdom, prosperity, happiness and success, especially during daily worship, Ganesh Chaturthi and other auspicious occasions.
