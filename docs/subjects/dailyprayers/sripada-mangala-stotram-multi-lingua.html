ఓం గురవే నమః
Shri Pada Shri Vallabha Siddha Mangala Stotram
శ్రీ పాద శ్రీ వల్లభ సిద్ధ మంగళ స్తోత్రం

A devotional stotram in praise of Shri Pada Shri Vallabha, the revered Siddha saint of Pithapuram, extolling his divine lineage, his mother Sumati, and the grace of the Dattatreya tradition. Each verse invokes his victory and universal glory through the refrain "Jaya Vijayibhava Digvijayibhava." Traditionally recited for blessings, protection and spiritual strength, it celebrates the saint as the embodiment of divine compassion and the fruit of accumulated merit.
