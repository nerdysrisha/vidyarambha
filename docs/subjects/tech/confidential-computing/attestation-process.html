Attestation Process

Notes on the custom attestation flow — how a workload proves it's running inside a genuine, unmodified TEE before it's handed secrets/keys.

Overview

Steps

1. Workload requests a quote from the TEE
2. Quote sent to attestation service
3. Attestation service verifies quote against known-good measurements
4. On success, secrets/keys released to workload
