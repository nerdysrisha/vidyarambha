2. Verbos básicos (Basic verbs)

This page introduces the three most essential Portuguese verbs — the foundation for everyday sentences. Ser and estar both translate as "to be" but are used in different situations (identity and permanent traits vs. temporary states — the distinction is covered in detail in Section 5), while ter means "to have". You'll also meet há, an invariable form used to say "there is / there are".

This is a quick reference for the conjugations only. Detailed usage rules and exercises for each verb are in their own sections:ser → Section 3, estar → Section 4, ser vs. estar → Section 5, ter → Section 6.
