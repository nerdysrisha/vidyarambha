10. Tipos de verbos (Verb types)

Portuguese regular verbs fall into three groups according to their infinitive ending — -ar, -er, or -ir — and that ending decides which set of endings gets attached for every tense and person. Once you know the group a verb belongs to, you can conjugate it by following a fixed, predictable pattern.

There's also a tiny fourth group, -or, but it's really just pôr and its compounds — repor, compor, opor — and it doesn't follow a regular pattern at all. It's something to recognize by sight rather than conjugate by rule. The table below shows the shape of each group before we look at each one individually.

Padrão completo (Full pattern at a glance)

-erbeber, comer, escrever, fazer

Exemplos

Eu gosto de falar português.I like to speak Portuguese.

Nós vamos comer agora.We are going to eat now.

Eu quero ler.I want to read.

Eles vão partir amanhã.They are leaving tomorrow.

Eu preciso de dormir.I need to sleep.

Vou sair agora.I am going out now.

Vou pôr o livro aqui.I will put the book here.

Exercícios — Tipos de verbos

1. Qual é a terminação?

Identifique a terminação do infinitivo.

  1. falar -ar
  2. comer -er
  3. partir -ir
  4. pôr -or
  5. trabalhar -ar
  6. beber -er

2. Complete.

Complete com o verbo correto.

  1. Eu gosto de ___ português. (falar) falar
  2. Nós vamos ___ agora. (comer) comer
  3. Eu quero ___. (ler) ler
  4. Vou ___ agora. (sair) sair
  5. Eu preciso de ___. (dormir) dormir

3. Perguntas rápidas.

Perguntas de duas palavras e frases simples. As respostas ficam escondidas.

  1. Que terminação? — falar -ar
  2. Que terminação? — beber -er
  3. Que terminação? — dormir -ir
  4. Que verbo? — I want to read. ler
  5. Qual verbo? — I will put the book here. pôr
  6. Complete: Eu vou ___. sair
  7. Complete: Nós vamos ___. comer
  8. Which ending? — partir -ir
