16b. Verbo chamar-se (reflexivo) (The verb chamar-se — reflexive "to be called")

Chamar-se is a reflexive verb — the subject and the object are the same person, so alongside the verb I also need a reflexive pronoun (me, te, se).

This page also recaps ser and the definite article (o / a), since I reuse both constantly in the exercises below.

Presente do Indicativo — chamar-se (Present tense — to be called)

PronomeChamar-seEnglish
euchamo-meI am called... (first person)
tuchamas-teyou are called... (second person, informal)
você / ele / ela / o senhor / a senhorachama-seyou/he/she/you (formal) are/is called... (third person)

Colocação do pronome reflexo (Reflexive pronoun placement)

The reflexive pronoun (me, te, se) goes right after the question word and before the verb, as in Como se chama? In a statement with no question word, the pronoun normally attaches to the end of the verb instead, as in Chamo-me... / Chama-se.... I usually leave out the subject pronoun (eu, tu, etc.), since the verb ending already shows who's speaking.

Formal vs. informal (Formal vs. informal)

I use the 2nd person (tu) informally — Como te chamas? I use the 3rd person (você / o senhor / a senhora) as the normal, formal way to ask someone's name — Como se chama?

GéneroArtigoEnglish

In Portuguese, the definite article is frequently used before someone's first name, as in o Filipe, a Joana.

Exemplos

Perguntas — chamar-se

Como se chama?What is your name? (formal)

Como te chamas?What is your name? (informal)

Respostas — chamar-se

Chamo-me Ana Ribeiro.My name is Ana Ribeiro.

O Filipe chama-se Filipe Costa.Filipe's name is Filipe Costa.

A Joana chama-se Joana Alves.Joana's name is Joana Alves.

Ser e artigo definido — revisão

Sou o Pedro.I am Pedro.

És a Teresa?Are you Teresa?

B. Complete as frases com me, te ou se antes ou depois do verbo.

Some blanks stay empty — the pronoun only fills one of the two positions, never both.
