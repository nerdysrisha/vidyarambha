1. Artigos (Articles)

Artigos definidos (Definite articles)

o (the) (वह), os (the, pl.) (वे), a (the) (वह), as (the, pl.) (वे)

Artigos indefinidos (Indefinite articles)

um (a/an) (एक), uns (some) (कुछ), uma (a/an) (एक), umas (some) (कुछ)

Basic idea: The article changes according to gender and number.
