9. Expressões (Expressions)

Expressões are fixed, everyday phrases — greetings, polite requests, good wishes, and small talk. They don't follow regular grammar rules on their own; each one is simply learned as a set phrase. This page groups them by function, with the English equivalent given next to every entry.
