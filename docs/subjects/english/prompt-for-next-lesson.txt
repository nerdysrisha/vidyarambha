 
**Reusable prompt**

I am making lesson [NUMBER] for my Class 4 students. Below is the lesson text, with [IMAGE: ...] notes where a picture belongs. Build lesson-[NUMBER].json using the same structure as lessons 10, 11 and 12.

**Structure**
- Top level: `"title"` and `"blocks"`.
- Each block has `"context"` (a list of `{ "paras": [...], "image": "[NUMBER]-p#.webp" }`) and `"sections"`.
- Each section is `{ "type": "qa", "heading": "...", "note": "..." (only if needed), "items": [ { "q": "...", "a": "..." } ] }`.
- Number images in order of use: `[NUMBER]-p1.webp`, `[NUMBER]-p2.webp`, and so on.
- Put images only in `context` blocks, never on individual items. Group small pictures (for example, one per exercise item) into one nearby context image.
- Keep all original content: stories, poems, exercises, new words and sight words.
- Split stories or poems into context chunks at the picture positions.

**Answers**
- Give real answers wherever there is one correct answer.
- For open-ended questions, write a "Teacher note:" with guidance or sample answers.
- For picture-based questions you cannot see, give teacher guidance only.
- If something needs a table, grid or drawing, add a teacher note saying where I will add the image. Do not try to draw it in JSON.
- Fix small errors in the original text, and tell me what you changed.

**Learning additions**
- Add light touches of physics or science and maths, linked to the lesson theme. Put them in sections called "Science Corner" and "Maths Corner", with a short hands-on activity and teacher notes where useful.
- Keep language simple for Class 4.

**Output**
- Create the file as lesson-[NUMBER].json and present it.
- Then briefly summarise the blocks, the images, and anything you changed.
- Do not ask me questions. Use your best judgement and tell me your assumptions.

**Title:** [your title, or "suggest one"]

**Lesson text:**
[paste lesson text here]

---

**Optional extras**
- To get the image prompts at the same time, add: "Also give me an image prompt for each image, with one common paragraph first."
- To change the amount of science and maths, add: "Make the science and maths bigger than last time" or "keep it very light".
- To get a plan before the build, add: "Give me your opinions first and do not build anything."
