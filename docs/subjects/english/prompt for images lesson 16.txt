Image prompts

Common paragraph (paste at the start of every prompt):

Warm children’s storybook illustration for Class 4 (ages 9 to 10), soft watercolour-and-gouache texture, rounded friendly shapes, gentle outlines, bright but not neon colours (saffron, teal, sky blue, warm yellow, leaf green), soft light. Landscape 3:2. The picture is a collage of the panels listed, divided by slanted diagonal white gutters, like a comic page. Each panel shows one clear scene with a little breathing space, and all panels share the same palette. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere. Keep these characters identical in every image: Rani (8-year-old girl, long black braid with a red ribbon, orange skirt, red top, big curious eyes); Pratik (her elder brother, about 12, short hair, blue T-shirt, khaki shorts); Vivaan (his friend, about 11, round glasses, yellow T-shirt); Nandini Aunty (Vivaan’s mother, a scientist, green sari, kind smile, hair in a neat bun); Amma (Rani’s mother, maroon cotton sari); a brown-and-white village dog. Child-friendly and cheerful throughout.

p1 (3 panels)

Large left panel: Rani on a village lane at dusk, one finger pointing up at the sky, looking curious.
Top-right panel: Amma sitting outside the house, winnowing grain in a flat basket, with the dog resting nearby. Rani stands beside her looking up at a crescent Moon.
Bottom-right panel: morning bedroom. Rani sits up in bed, surprised, while Pratik points excitedly at a TV showing a spacecraft on the Moon.

p2 (3 panels)

Panel 1: Rani and Pratik watch a TV that shows the Chandrayaan-3 lander on grey Moon ground, with a small saffron-white-green flag.
Panel 2: Rani and Pratik walk down a sunny village lane towards a house.
Panel 3: Nandini Aunty and Vivaan welcome the children at the door of a room full of rocket models and posters.

p3 (3 panels)

Panel 1: Nandini Aunty hands out glasses of sharbat while Rani, Pratik and Vivaan listen.
Panel 2: Rani points at a shelf of rocket models, and Pratik grins.
Panel 3: a tall rocket poster in tricolour stripes. Rani jumps with joy, and Nandini Aunty smiles.

p4 (3 panels)

Panel 1: a child in a cosy sleeping bag lying on a giant crescent Moon holding a blank banner, and another child in a spacesuit flying beside a rocket.
Panel 2: a red car with luggage on the roof and a happy family waving.
Panel 3: a grandfather on a verandah telling a travel story to a grandchild, with a small thought bubble showing a train and mountains.

p5 (4 panels)

Panel 1: a tiny mouse holding a small balloon and a big elephant holding a big balloon (no letters on the balloons).
Panel 2: a dog barking at a postman on a cycle.
Panel 3: a woman giving chapattis to a cow.
Panel 4: a sparrow building a nest in a mango tree.

p6 (4 panels)

Panel 1: an eagle perched on top of a building.
Panel 2: a woman holding an umbrella in monsoon rain.
Panel 3: an ice-cream man with his cart on an evening street.
Panel 4: a girl in a farmyard holding an apricot, a chikoo and an orange, with a brown hen and a fresh egg at her feet.

p7 (4 panels)

Panel 1: a park by a river, with swings on one side and children skipping stones on the other.
Panel 2: a steaming bowl of soup with a spoon.
Panel 3: a shepherd boy on a hill shouting, with sheep and a wolf far in the distance.
Panel 4: a blue skirt and a pink shirt on a clothesline.

p8 (3 panels)

Panel 1: a wooden hanging sign with seven small coloured tags (blank) and pink flowers.
Panel 2: six children in a line, with the first one proudly at the front and the last one waving.
Panel 3: a girl pointing at a wall calendar with one day circled in red.

p9 (3 panels)

Panel 1: a classroom where a balloon “rocket” zooms along a string tied between two chairs while children cheer.
Panel 2: on the Moon, a child leaps very high in a spacesuit, while a child on Earth beside it jumps low.
Panel 3: two astronauts on the Moon. One waves his arms trying to shout, the other taps his shoulder and points to the radio on his helmet.

p10 (4 panels)

Panel 1: children watching a balloon puff up on a bottle, with bubbles inside (fizzy balloon).
Panel 2: a teacher placing a glass jar over a small lit candle, with children watching from a safe distance.
Panel 3: a tray of flour with cocoa dust and marble craters, while a child drops a marble.
Panel 4: the Vikram lander on the Moon with a small six-wheeled rover rolling down its ramp, and the flag nearby.

p11 (3 panels)

Panel 1: a classroom floor with a table-tennis ball, a small marble and a long string between them, and children lying down beside it to look.
Panel 2: two calendar pages (July and August) with one date circled on each and a tiny rocket flying between them. Leave the pages blank, with no printed numbers.
Panel 3: children building a model rocket from a tin (cylinder), a paper cone and three card triangles for fins.

p12 (3 panels)

Panel 1: children cutting newspaper clippings on a classroom floor.
Panel 2: two children holding up a green chart paper pasted with a rocket, a Moon and a flag.
Panel 3: a girl reading a storybook to a friendly crescent-moon character wearing a wizard hat.

p13 (2 panels)

Panel 1: Rani holding two balloons, a green one with a tick and a red one with a cross (symbols only, no letters).
Panel 2: a rooftop at night. Rani, Pratik and Vivaan wave at a glowing Moon, and Vivaan holds a toy rocket.
