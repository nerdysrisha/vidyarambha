Tatra Shrirvijayo Bhutirdhruva Nitirmatirmama ||
Shribhagavanuvacha
Ananyashchintayanto Mam Ye Janaha Paryupasate |
Tesham Nityabhiyuktanam Yogakshemam Vahamyaham ||
Paritranaya Sadhunam Vinashaya Cha Dushkritam |
Dharmasamsthapanarthaya Sambhavami Yuge Yuge ||
Arta Vishannaha Shithilashcha Bhitaha
Ghoreshu Cha Vyadhishu Vartamanaha |
Sankirtya Narayanashabdamatram
Vimuktaduhkhaha Sukhino Bhavantu ||
Kayena Vacha Manasendriyairva
Buddhyatmana Va Prakritehe Svabhavat |
Karomi Yadyat Sakalam Parasmai
Narayanayeti Samarpayami ||

Recited daily at dawn or dusk, or on Ekadashi, for peace and protection. Traditionally preceded by the Dhyana Shlokas and Nyasa, and closed with the Phalashruti (the verses on the benefits of recitation) above.
