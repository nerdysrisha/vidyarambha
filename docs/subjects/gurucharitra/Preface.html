Preface

About Sri Guru Charitra

The Book

Sri Guru Charitra is a devotional scripture about two revered incarnations of Lord Dattatreya, Sripada Srivallabha and Narasimha Saraswati. It tells of their lives, their miracles and their teachings, and of the devotees whose lives they changed. It was composed in Marathi verse and is read and recited by devotees to this day.

The Author

The work is traditionally attributed to Saraswati Gangadhar, who belonged to the lineage of Sayamdev, a disciple of Narasimha Saraswati. It is generally dated to around the 15th–16th century.

Who's Who

  • DattatreyaThe divine teacher (guru) of the tradition, whose presence the two incarnations are believed to carry.
  • Sripada SrivallabhaThe first incarnation, born at Pithapuram, who later lived in seclusion at Kuruvapur on the Krishna river.
  • Narasimha SaraswatiThe second incarnation, born at Karanja, who became an ascetic, travelled widely and settled at Gangapur. Most of the book is about him.
  • SiddhaA disciple of Narasimha Saraswati. He tells the story, speaking of what he saw and heard from his guru.
  • NamdharakThe seeker who asks Siddha about the guru. His questions carry the story from one chapter to the next.

The Places

  • PithapuramBirthplace of Sripada Srivallabha, in present-day Andhra Pradesh.
  • KuruvapurAn island in the Krishna river where Sripada Srivallabha spent his later years.
  • KaranjaBirthplace of Narasimha Saraswati, in present-day Maharashtra.
  • GangapurOn the Bhima river, in present-day Karnataka. Narasimha Saraswati lived here for many years, and it remains the main centre of devotion.
  • Audumbar and NarasobawadiQuiet places on the banks of the Krishna where he stayed and taught.
  • SrisailamThe sacred hill from which, according to tradition, he withdrew from the world.
